Personal media hub for Apple devices

AllPlayer

AllPlayer helps you play, manage, and save your video content across Apple devices. Play local files, browse your Photos library, connect to LAN servers, add online video stream URLs, cache live streams, choose stream quality, and watch two streams side by side.

Get Support View Privacy Policy

Key Features

Built for personal libraries, NAS playback, m3u8 testing, and live streams.

Local Video Playback

Play common formats including MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, FLV, WMV, TS, M4V, RMVB, and RM.

Photos Library Support

Browse and play videos from your system Photos library, and view images inside the app.

LAN Media Library

Connect to SMB, WebDAV, and FTP servers to browse folders and play network media files directly.

Video Stream Playback

Add m3u8, HLS, live stream, or web video URLs, then manage your stream list with search, sorting, sharing, import, and export.

Live Stream Caching

Save currently playing streams locally for later viewing when the source and network conditions allow it.

Quality Selection

Switch between available stream qualities when multiple resolutions are detected.

Dual Stream Playback

Watch two video streams side by side on iPad or iPhone in landscape mode.

File Management

Search, sort, create folders, delete, copy, move, and rename files in your personal media library.

Playback Controls

Control playback speed, aspect ratio, seeking, full screen, sharing, and AirPlay.

Use Cases

One app for local files, home servers, and stream workflows.

  • Manage personal video collections, course videos, and offline media.
  • Play media from NAS devices, home servers, and LAN shared folders.
  • Add, organize, and test m3u8, HLS, and live stream URLs.
  • Cache currently playing live streams for later viewing.
  • Enjoy an immersive cinema-style experience on Apple Vision Pro.

Key Features

为个人片库、NAS 播放、m3u8 测试与直播观看设计。

本地视频播放

播放 MP4、MOV、MKV、AVI、FLV、WMV、TS、M4V、RMVB、RM 等常见格式。

照片图库支持

浏览并播放系统 Photos 图库中的视频，也可以在应用内查看图片。

局域网媒体库

连接 SMB、WebDAV 和 FTP 服务器，浏览远程文件夹并直接播放网络媒体文件。

在线视频流播放

添加 m3u8、HLS、直播流或网页视频 URL，并支持搜索、排序、分享、导入和导出。

直播缓存

在源站和网络条件允许时，将正在播放的视频流保存到本地以便稍后观看。

清晰度选择

检测到多档分辨率时，可在可用清晰度之间切换。

双路分屏播放

在 iPad 或 iPhone 横屏时并排观看两个视频流。

文件管理

搜索、排序、新建文件夹、删除、复制、移动和重命名文件。

高级播放控制

调节播放速度、画面比例、进度跳转、全屏、分享和 AirPlay。

Use Cases

覆盖本地文件、家庭服务器和在线视频流工作流。

  • 管理个人视频收藏、课程视频和离线资料。
  • 播放 NAS、家庭服务器或局域网共享目录中的媒体。
  • 添加、整理并测试 m3u8、HLS 和直播源地址。
  • 缓存正在播放的直播流，稍后继续观看。
  • 在 Apple Vision Pro 上享受沉浸式影院观看体验。
