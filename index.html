Connect from more places

At home, away, or casting to another screen.

Connection tools are now part of the product experience, not a collection of settings you have to troubleshoot alone.

↗

Built-in Tailscale

Connect to your Tailscale network to access compatible home media services while away, without running a separate Tailscale app on the playback device.

◎

Connection assistant

Discover sources, follow guided setup, and test both the current network and real server download speed when playback needs diagnosis.

◫

Cast and receive

Send supported media with AirPlay, or let AllPlayer appear as a DLNA / UPnP target. AirPlay receiving is also available on supported iPhone and Mac configurations.
