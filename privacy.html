Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026. AllPlayer is a video player application developed by an independent developer and distributed exclusively on Apple platforms.

Privacy by Design

AllPlayer does not require an account or personal information for core playback features. The app mainly accesses and plays media locally on your device, unless you choose to use network playback, LAN connections, support email, or system diagnostics.

Information Collection and Use

If you contact us by email at playersupport.arco@gmail.com, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message. If optional feedback features are added in the future, submitted information will only be used for the stated purpose.

Local Data and File Access

AllPlayer may access videos, images, folders, or connected services only to play, browse, manage, or save media you choose. We do not upload, store, or share your local media files on external servers.

  • Photos library access is used to browse and play selected media.
  • SMB, WebDAV, FTP, HLS, m3u8, live stream, and web video URLs are accessed only when you initiate those features.
  • Live stream caching saves the current stream locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

AllPlayer is distributed through Apple platforms and may rely on Apple-provided services for App Store distribution, payments, diagnostics, and crash reporting. These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Log Data

When an error occurs, limited technical data may be collected through platform-provided tools, such as device model, operating system version, app configuration, and time of use. This information is used only to debug issues and improve app stability.

Cookies and Links

AllPlayer does not directly use cookies. Third-party services or embedded components may use similar technologies. AllPlayer may contain links to external services or websites, and their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable means to protect information, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

AllPlayer is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Questions can be sent to playersupport.arco@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 5 月 1 日。AllPlayer 是由独立开发者提供、仅在 Apple 平台分发的视频播放器应用。

隐私优先

AllPlayer 的核心播放功能不要求你创建账户，也不要求你主动提供个人信息。应用主要在你的设备本地访问和播放媒体内容，除非你主动使用网络播放、局域网连接、问题反馈或系统诊断等功能。

信息收集与使用

如果你通过 playersupport.arco@gmail.com 联系我们，我们可能会收到你的邮箱地址以及邮件中主动提供的内容。如果未来加入反馈表单等功能，你提交的信息只会用于当时明确说明的目的。

本地数据与文件访问

AllPlayer 可能访问视频、图片、文件目录或你连接的服务，这些访问仅用于播放、浏览、管理或保存你主动选择的媒体内容。我们不会将你的本地媒体文件上传、存储或分享至外部服务器。

  • 照片图库访问用于浏览和播放你授权的媒体。
  • SMB、WebDAV、FTP、HLS、m3u8、直播源和网页视频 URL 只会在你主动使用相关功能时访问。
  • 直播缓存会将当前播放的流保存到你的设备本地。

第三方服务

AllPlayer 通过 Apple 平台分发，并可能依赖 Apple 提供的 App Store 分发、支付、诊断和崩溃报告等系统服务。这些服务可能根据各自的隐私政策处理信息。

日志数据

当应用发生错误时，我们可能通过平台提供的工具获得有限技术数据，例如设备型号、操作系统版本、错误发生时的应用配置、使用时间等。这些信息仅用于调试问题和提升稳定性。

Cookies 与外部链接

AllPlayer 不会直接使用 Cookies。部分第三方服务或嵌入式组件可能使用类似技术。应用可能包含外部服务或网站链接，其隐私实践由各自政策约束。

数据安全

我们努力采用商业上合理的方式保护相关信息，但互联网传输和电子存储不存在绝对安全的方法。

儿童隐私

AllPlayer 不面向 18 岁以下儿童，我们不会故意收集儿童的个人身份信息。

你的隐私权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理、请求数据可携带或撤回同意。你可以通过邮箱联系我们行使这些权利。

政策更新与联系我们

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都会发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。如有问题，请联系 playersupport.arco@gmail.com。
